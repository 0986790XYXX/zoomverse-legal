Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Zoomverse respects and protects your privacy.

1. Data storage

Every canvas and artwork you create is stored only on this device. We do not upload, collect, or share your creative content with any third party.

2. What we don't collect

The app requires no account and collects no personal information such as your name, email, location, or contacts.

3. System permissions

When you share a piece, the system share sheet is used; your artwork is handed only to the target app you choose, at the moment you share.

4. Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages, contains no targeted ads, and collects no personal information from children.

5. Contact

For any privacy questions, contact: support@douhouse.com
